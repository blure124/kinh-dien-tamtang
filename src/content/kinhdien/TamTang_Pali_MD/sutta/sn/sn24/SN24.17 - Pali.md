# Saṁyutta Nikāya 24.17

# 1. Sotāpattivagga

# Hoticanacahotitathāgatosutta

**sn24.17:1.1** Sāvatthinidānaṁ.

**sn24.17:1.2** “Kismiṁ nu kho, bhikkhave, sati, kiṁ upādāya, kiṁ abhinivissa evaṁ diṭṭhi uppajjati:

**sn24.17:1.3** ‘hoti ca na ca hoti tathāgato paraṁ maraṇā’”ti?

**sn24.17:1.4** “Bhagavaṁmūlakā no, bhante, dhammā …pe…

**sn24.17:1.5** niyato sambodhiparāyano”ti.

**sn24.17:1.6** Sattarasamaṁ.
