# Saṁyutta Nikāya 50.13

# 2. Appamādavagga

# Appamādavagga

**sn50.13-22:1.1** (Appamādavaggo vitthāretabbo.)

**sn50.13-22:2.0** Tassuddānaṁ

**sn50.13-22:2.1** Tathāgataṁ padaṁ kūṭaṁ,

**sn50.13-22:2.2** mūlaṁ sārena vassikaṁ;

**sn50.13-22:2.3** Rājā candimasūriyā,

**sn50.13-22:2.4** vatthena dasamaṁ padanti.
