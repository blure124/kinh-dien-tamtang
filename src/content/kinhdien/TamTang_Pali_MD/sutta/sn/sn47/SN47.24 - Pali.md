# Saṁyutta Nikāya 47.24

# 3. Sīlaṭṭhitivagga

# Suddhasutta

**sn47.24:1.1** Sāvatthinidānaṁ.

**sn47.24:1.2** “Cattārome, bhikkhave, satipaṭṭhānā.

**sn47.24:1.3** Katame cattāro?

**sn47.24:1.4** Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu kāye kāyānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā, vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṁ;

**sn47.24:1.5** vedanāsu …pe…

**sn47.24:1.6** citte …pe…

**sn47.24:1.7** dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā, vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṁ.

**sn47.24:1.8** Ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro satipaṭṭhānā”ti.

**sn47.24:1.9** Catutthaṁ.
