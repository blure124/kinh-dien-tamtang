# Saṁyutta Nikāya 52.13

# 2. Dutiyavagga

# Dibbasotasutta

**sn52.13:1.1** “Imesañca panāhaṁ, āvuso, catunnaṁ satipaṭṭhānānaṁ bhāvitattā bahulīkatattā dibbāya sotadhātuyā visuddhāya atikkantamānusikāya ubho sadde suṇāmi dibbe ca mānuse ca ye dūre santike cā”ti.

**sn52.13:1.2** Tatiyaṁ.
