# Saṁyutta Nikāya 52.12

# 2. Dutiyavagga

# Iddhividhasutta

**sn52.12:1.1** “Imesañca panāhaṁ, āvuso, catunnaṁ satipaṭṭhānānaṁ bhāvitattā bahulīkatattā anekavihitaṁ iddhividhaṁ paccanubhomi—ekopi hutvā bahudhā homi …pe… yāva brahmalokāpi kāyena vasaṁ vattemī”ti.

**sn52.12:1.2** Dutiyaṁ.
