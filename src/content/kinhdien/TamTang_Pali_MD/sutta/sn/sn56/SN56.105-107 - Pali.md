# Saṁyutta Nikāya 56.105–107

# 11. Pañcagatipeyyālavagga

# Manussacutidevanirayādisutta

**sn56.105-107:1.1** … “Evameva kho, bhikkhave, appakā te sattā ye manussā cutā devesu paccājāyanti; atha kho eteva bahutarā sattā ye manussā cutā niraye paccājāyanti …pe… tiracchānayoniyā paccājāyanti …pe… pettivisaye paccājāyanti …pe….

**sn56.105-107:1.2** Chaṭṭhaṁ.
