# Saṁyutta Nikāya 37.6

# 1. Paṭhamapeyyālavagga

# Upanāhīsutta

**sn37.6:1.1** “Pañcahi, anuruddha, dhammehi samannāgato mātugāmo kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā apāyaṁ duggatiṁ vinipātaṁ nirayaṁ upapajjati.

**sn37.6:1.2** Katamehi pañcahi?

**sn37.6:1.3** Assaddho ca hoti, ahiriko ca hoti, anottappī ca hoti, upanāhī ca hoti, duppañño ca hoti—

**sn37.6:1.4** imehi kho, anuruddha, pañcahi dhammehi samannāgato mātugāmo kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā apāyaṁ duggatiṁ vinipātaṁ nirayaṁ upapajjatī”ti.

**sn37.6:1.5** Chaṭṭhaṁ.
