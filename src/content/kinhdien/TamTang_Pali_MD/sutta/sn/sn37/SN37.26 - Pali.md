# Saṁyutta Nikāya 37.26

# 3. Balavagga

# Pasayhasutta

**sn37.26:1.1** “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, mātugāmassa balāni.

**sn37.26:1.2** Katamāni pañca?

**sn37.26:1.3** Rūpabalaṁ, bhogabalaṁ, ñātibalaṁ, puttabalaṁ, sīlabalaṁ—

**sn37.26:1.4** imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañca mātugāmassa balāni.

**sn37.26:1.5** Imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahi balehi samannāgato mātugāmo sāmikaṁ pasayha agāraṁ ajjhāvasatī”ti.

**sn37.26:1.6** Dutiyaṁ.
