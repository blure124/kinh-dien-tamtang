# Saṁyutta Nikāya 55.44

# 5. Sagāthakapuññābhisandavagga

# Paṭhamamahaddhanasutta

**sn55.44:1.1** “Catūhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato ariyasāvako ‘aḍḍho mahaddhano mahābhogo’ti vuccati.

**sn55.44:2.1** Katamehi catūhi?

**sn55.44:2.2** Idha, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako buddhe aveccappasādena samannāgato hoti—

**sn55.44:2.3** itipi so bhagavā …pe… satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavāti;

**sn55.44:2.4** dhamme …pe…

**sn55.44:2.5** saṅghe …pe…

**sn55.44:2.6** ariyakantehi sīlehi samannāgato hoti akhaṇḍehi …pe… samādhisaṁvattanikehi.

**sn55.44:2.7** Imehi kho, bhikkhave, catūhi dhammehi samannāgato ariyasāvako ‘aḍḍho mahaddhano mahābhogo’ti vuccatī”ti.

**sn55.44:2.8** Catutthaṁ.
