# Saṁyutta Nikāya 55.55

# 6. Sappaññavagga

# Sotāpattiphalasutta

**sn55.55:1.1** “Cattārome, bhikkhave, dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya saṁvattanti.

**sn55.55:1.2** Katame cattāro?

**sn55.55:1.3** Sappurisasaṁsevo, saddhammassavanaṁ, yonisomanasikāro, dhammānudhammappaṭipatti—

**sn55.55:1.4** ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā sotāpattiphalasacchikiriyāya saṁvattantī”ti.

**sn55.55:1.5** Pañcamaṁ.
