# Saṁyutta Nikāya 22.6

# 1. Nakulapituvagga

# Paṭisallāṇasutta

**sn22.6:1.1** Sāvatthinidānaṁ.

**sn22.6:1.2** “Paṭisallāṇe, bhikkhave, yogamāpajjatha.

**sn22.6:1.3** Paṭisallīṇo, bhikkhave, bhikkhu yathābhūtaṁ pajānāti.

**sn22.6:1.4** Kiñca yathābhūtaṁ pajānāti?

**sn22.6:1.5** Rūpassa samudayañca atthaṅgamañca, vedanāya samudayañca atthaṅgamañca, saññāya samudayañca atthaṅgamañca, saṅkhārānaṁ samudayañca atthaṅgamañca, viññāṇassa samudayañca atthaṅgamañca …pe…

**sn22.6:1.6** (yathā paṭhamasutte tathā vitthāretabbo.)

**sn22.6:1.7** Chaṭṭhaṁ.
