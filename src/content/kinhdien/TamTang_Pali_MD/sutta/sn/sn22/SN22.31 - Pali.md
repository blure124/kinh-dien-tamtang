# Saṁyutta Nikāya 22.31

# 3. Bhāravagga

# Aghamūlasutta

**sn22.31:1.1** Sāvatthinidānaṁ.

**sn22.31:1.2** “Aghañca, bhikkhave, desessāmi aghamūlañca.

**sn22.31:1.3** Taṁ suṇātha.

**sn22.31:1.4** Katamañca, bhikkhave, aghaṁ?

**sn22.31:1.5** Rūpaṁ, bhikkhave, aghaṁ, vedanā aghaṁ, saññā aghaṁ, saṅkhārā aghaṁ, viññāṇaṁ aghaṁ.

**sn22.31:1.6** Idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, aghaṁ.

**sn22.31:1.7** Katamañca, bhikkhave, aghamūlaṁ?

**sn22.31:1.8** Yāyaṁ taṇhā ponobhavikā nandīrāgasahagatā tatratatrābhinandinī;

**sn22.31:1.9** Seyyathidaṁ—kāmataṇhā, bhavataṇhā, vibhavataṇhā.

**sn22.31:1.10** Idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, aghamūlan”ti.

**sn22.31:1.11** Dasamaṁ.
