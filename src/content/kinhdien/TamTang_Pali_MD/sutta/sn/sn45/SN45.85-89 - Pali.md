# Saṁyutta Nikāya 45.85–89

# 8. Dutiyaekadhammapeyyālavagga

# Dutiyasīlasampadādisuttapañcaka

**sn45.85-89:1.1** “Nāhaṁ, bhikkhave, aññaṁ ekadhammampi samanupassāmi, yena anuppanno vā ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo uppajjati, uppanno vā ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvanāpāripūriṁ gacchati, yathayidaṁ, bhikkhave, sīlasampadā …pe…

**sn45.85-89:1.2** yathayidaṁ, bhikkhave, chandasampadā …pe…

**sn45.85-89:1.3** yathayidaṁ, bhikkhave, attasampadā …pe…

**sn45.85-89:1.4** yathayidaṁ, bhikkhave, diṭṭhisampadā …pe…

**sn45.85-89:1.5** yathayidaṁ, bhikkhave, appamādasampadā …pe….

**sn45.85-89:1.6** Chaṭṭhaṁ.
