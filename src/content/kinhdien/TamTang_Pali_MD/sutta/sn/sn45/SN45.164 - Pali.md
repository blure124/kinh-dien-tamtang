# Saṁyutta Nikāya 45.164

# 13. Esanāvagga

# Bhavasutta

**sn45.164:1.1** “Tayome, bhikkhave, bhavā.

**sn45.164:1.2** Katame tayo?

**sn45.164:1.3** Kāmabhavo, rūpabhavo, arūpabhavo—

**sn45.164:1.4** ime kho, bhikkhave, tayo bhavā.

**sn45.164:1.5** Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, tiṇṇannaṁ bhavānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya …pe… ayaṁ ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabbo”ti.

**sn45.164:1.6** Catutthaṁ.
