# Saṁyutta Nikāya 45.173

# 14. Oghavagga

# Upādānasutta

**sn45.173:1.1** “Cattārimāni, bhikkhave, upādānāni.

**sn45.173:1.2** Katamāni cattāri?

**sn45.173:1.3** Kāmupādānaṁ, diṭṭhupādānaṁ, sīlabbatupādānaṁ, attavādupādānaṁ—

**sn45.173:1.4** imāni kho, bhikkhave, cattāri upādānāni.

**sn45.173:1.5** Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, catunnaṁ upādānānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya …pe… ayaṁ ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabbo”ti.

**sn45.173:1.6** Tatiyaṁ.
