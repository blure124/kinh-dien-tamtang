# Saṁyutta Nikāya 23.6

# 1. Paṭhamamāravagga

# Dutiyasamaṇasutta

**sn23.6:1.1** Sāvatthinidānaṁ.

**sn23.6:1.2** Ekamantaṁ nisinnaṁ kho āyasmantaṁ rādhaṁ bhagavā etadavoca:

**sn23.6:1.3** “pañcime, rādha, upādānakkhandhā.

**sn23.6:1.4** Katame pañca?

**sn23.6:1.5** Rūpupādānakkhandho …pe… viññāṇupādānakkhandho.

**sn23.6:1.6** Ye hi keci, rādha, samaṇā vā brāhmaṇā vā imesaṁ pañcannaṁ upādānakkhandhānaṁ samudayañca atthaṅgamañca assādañca ādīnavañca nissaraṇañca yathābhūtaṁ nappajānanti …pe…

**sn23.6:1.7** sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharantī”ti.

**sn23.6:1.8** Chaṭṭhaṁ.
