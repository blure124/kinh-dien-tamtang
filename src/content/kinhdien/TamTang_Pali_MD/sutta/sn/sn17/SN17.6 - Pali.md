# Saṁyutta Nikāya 17.6

# 1. Paṭhamavagga

# Asanisutta

**sn17.6:1.1** Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati.

**sn17.6:1.2** “Dāruṇo, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko …pe… adhigamāya.

**sn17.6:1.3** Kaṁ, bhikkhave, asanivicakkaṁ āgacchatu, sekhaṁ appattamānasaṁ lābhasakkārasiloko anupāpuṇātu.

**sn17.6:2.1** Asanivicakkanti kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasilokassetaṁ adhivacanaṁ.

**sn17.6:2.2** Evaṁ dāruṇo kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko …pe…

**sn17.6:2.3** evañhi vo, bhikkhave, sikkhitabban”ti.

**sn17.6:2.4** Chaṭṭhaṁ.
