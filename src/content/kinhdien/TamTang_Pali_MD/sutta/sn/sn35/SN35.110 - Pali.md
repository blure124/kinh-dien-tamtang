# Saṁyutta Nikāya 35.110

# 11. Yogakkhemivagga

# Upādāniyasutta

**sn35.110:1.1** “Upādāniye ca, bhikkhave, dhamme desessāmi upādānañca.

**sn35.110:1.2** Taṁ suṇātha.

**sn35.110:1.3** Katame ca, bhikkhave, upādāniyā dhammā, katamañca upādānaṁ?

**sn35.110:1.4** Cakkhuṁ, bhikkhave, upādāniyo dhammo.

**sn35.110:1.5** Yo tattha chandarāgo, taṁ tattha upādānaṁ …pe…

**sn35.110:1.6** jivhā upādāniyo dhammo …pe…

**sn35.110:1.7** mano upādāniyo dhammo.

**sn35.110:1.8** Yo tattha chandarāgo, taṁ tattha upādānaṁ.

**sn35.110:1.9** Ime vuccanti, bhikkhave, upādāniyā dhammā, idaṁ upādānan”ti.

**sn35.110:1.10** Sattamaṁ.
