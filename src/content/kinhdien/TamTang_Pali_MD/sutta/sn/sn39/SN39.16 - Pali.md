# Saṁyutta Nikāya 39.16

# 1. Sāmaṇḍakavagga

# Dukkarasutta

**sn39.16:1.1** “Kiṁ nu kho, āvuso sāriputta, imasmiṁ dhammavinaye dukkaran”ti?

**sn39.16:1.2** “Pabbajjā kho, āvuso, imasmiṁ dhammavinaye dukkarā”ti.

**sn39.16:1.3** “Pabbajitena panāvuso, kiṁ dukkaran”ti?

**sn39.16:1.4** “Pabbajitena kho, āvuso, abhirati dukkarā”ti.

**sn39.16:1.5** “Abhiratena panāvuso, kiṁ dukkaran”ti?

**sn39.16:1.6** “Abhiratena kho, āvuso, dhammānudhammappaṭipatti dukkarā”ti.

**sn39.16:1.7** “Kīvaciraṁ panāvuso, dhammānudhammappaṭipanno bhikkhu arahaṁ assā”ti?

**sn39.16:1.8** “Naciraṁ, āvuso”ti.

**sn39.16:1.9** Soḷasamaṁ.

**sn39.16:1.10** (Purimakasadisaṁ uddānaṁ.)

**sn39.16:1.11** Sāmaṇḍakasaṁyuttaṁ samattaṁ.
