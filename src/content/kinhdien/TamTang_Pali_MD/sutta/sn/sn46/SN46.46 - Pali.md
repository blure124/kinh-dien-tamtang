# Saṁyutta Nikāya 46.46

# 5. Cakkavattivagga

# Daliddasutta

**sn46.46:1.1** “‘Daliddo, daliddo’ti, bhante, vuccati.

**sn46.46:1.2** Kittāvatā nu kho, bhante, ‘daliddo’ti vuccatī”ti?

**sn46.46:1.3** “Sattannaṁ kho, bhikkhu, bojjhaṅgānaṁ abhāvitattā abahulīkatattā ‘daliddo’ti vuccati.

**sn46.46:1.4** Katamesaṁ sattannaṁ?

**sn46.46:1.5** Satisambojjhaṅgassa …pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgassa—

**sn46.46:1.6** imesaṁ kho, bhikkhu, sattannaṁ bojjhaṅgānaṁ abhāvitattā abahulīkatattā ‘daliddo’ti vuccatī”ti.

**sn46.46:1.7** Chaṭṭhaṁ.
