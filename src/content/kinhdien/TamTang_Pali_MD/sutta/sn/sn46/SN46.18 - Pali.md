# Saṁyutta Nikāya 46.18

# 2. Gilānavagga

# Viraddhasutta

**sn46.18:1.1** “Yesaṁ kesañci, bhikkhave, satta bojjhaṅgā viraddhā, viraddho tesaṁ ariyo maggo sammā dukkhakkhayagāmī.

**sn46.18:1.2** Yesaṁ kesañci, bhikkhave, satta bojjhaṅgā āraddhā, āraddho tesaṁ ariyo maggo sammā dukkhakkhayagāmī.

**sn46.18:1.3** Katame satta?

**sn46.18:1.4** Satisambojjhaṅgo …pe… upekkhāsambojjhaṅgo—

**sn46.18:1.5** yesaṁ kesañci, bhikkhave, ime satta bojjhaṅgā viraddhā, viraddho tesaṁ ariyo maggo sammā dukkhakkhayagāmī.

**sn46.18:1.6** Yesaṁ kesañci, bhikkhave, ime satta bojjhaṅgā āraddhā, āraddho tesaṁ ariyo maggo sammā dukkhakkhayagāmī”ti.

**sn46.18:1.7** Aṭṭhamaṁ.
