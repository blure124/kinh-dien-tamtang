# Saṁyutta Nikāya 2.12

# 2. Anāthapiṇḍikavagga

# Veṇḍusutta

**sn2.12:1.1** Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho veṇḍu devaputto bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi:

**sn2.12:2.1** “Sukhitāva te manujā,

**sn2.12:2.2** sugataṁ payirupāsiya;

**sn2.12:2.3** Yuñjaṁ gotamasāsane,

**sn2.12:2.4** appamattā nu sikkhare”ti.

**sn2.12:3.1** “Ye me pavutte siṭṭhipade,

**sn2.12:3.2** (veṇḍūti bhagavā)

**sn2.12:3.3** Anusikkhanti jhāyino;

**sn2.12:3.4** Kāle te appamajjantā,

**sn2.12:3.5** Na maccuvasagā siyun”ti.
