# Saṁyutta Nikāya 1.75

# 8. Chetvāvagga

# Bhītāsutta

**sn1.75:1.1** “Kiṁsūdha bhītā janatā anekā,

**sn1.75:1.2** Maggo canekāyatanappavutto;

**sn1.75:1.3** Pucchāmi taṁ gotama bhūripañña,

**sn1.75:1.4** Kismiṁ ṭhito paralokaṁ na bhāye”ti.

**sn1.75:2.1** “Vācaṁ manañca paṇidhāya sammā,

**sn1.75:2.2** Kāyena pāpāni akubbamāno;

**sn1.75:2.3** Bahvannapānaṁ gharamāvasanto,

**sn1.75:2.4** Saddho mudū saṁvibhāgī vadaññū;

**sn1.75:2.5** Etesu dhammesu ṭhito catūsu,

**sn1.75:2.6** Dhamme ṭhito paralokaṁ na bhāye”ti.
