# Milindapañha

# Arūpadhammavavatthānavagga

# 13. Samuddaekarasapañha

**mil3.7.13:1.1** Rājā āha—

**mil3.7.13:1.2** “bhante nāgasena, kena kāraṇena samuddo ekaraso loṇaraso”ti?

**mil3.7.13:1.3** “Cirasaṇṭhitattā kho, mahārāja, udakassa samuddo ekaraso loṇaraso”ti.

**mil3.7.13:2.1** “Kallosi, bhante nāgasenā”ti.

**mil3.7.13:3.1** Samuddaekarasapañho terasamo.
