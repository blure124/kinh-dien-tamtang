# Milindapañha

# Arūpadhammavavatthānavagga

# 12. Samuddapañha

**mil3.7.12:1.1** Rājā āha—

**mil3.7.12:1.2** “bhante nāgasena, ‘samuddo samuddo’ti vuccati, kena kāraṇena udakaṁ ‘samuddo’ti vuccatī”ti?

**mil3.7.12:1.3** Thero āha—

**mil3.7.12:1.4** “yattakaṁ, mahārāja, udakaṁ, tattakaṁ loṇaṁ.

**mil3.7.12:1.5** Yattakaṁ loṇaṁ, tattakaṁ udakaṁ.

**mil3.7.12:1.6** Tasmā ‘samuddo’ti vuccatī”ti.

**mil3.7.12:2.1** “Kallosi, bhante nāgasenā”ti.

**mil3.7.12:3.1** Samuddapañho dvādasamo.
