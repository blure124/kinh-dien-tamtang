# Milindapañha

# Vicāravagga

# 13. Vitakkalakkhaṇapañha

**mil3.3.13:1.1** “Bhante nāgasena, kiṁlakkhaṇo vitakko”ti?

**mil3.3.13:1.2** “Appanālakkhaṇo, mahārāja, vitakko”ti.

**mil3.3.13:2.1** “Opammaṁ karohī”ti.

**mil3.3.13:2.2** “Yathā, mahārāja, vaḍḍhakī suparikammakataṁ dāruṁ sandhismiṁ appeti;

**mil3.3.13:2.3** evameva kho, mahārāja, appanālakkhaṇo vitakko”ti.

**mil3.3.13:3.1** “Kallosi, bhante nāgasenā”ti.

**mil3.3.13:4.1** Vitakkalakkhaṇapañho terasamo.
