# Netti

# Paṭiniddesavāra

# Sampāta 6

# Catubyūhahārasampāta

**ne25:1.1** Tattha katamo catubyūho hārasampāto.

**ne25:2.1** “Tasmā rakkhitacittassa, sammāsaṅkappagocaro”ti gāthā.

**ne25:2.2** “Tasmā rakkhitacittassā”ti rakkhitaṁ paripālīyatīti esā nirutti.

**ne25:2.3** Idha bhagavato ko adhippāyo?

**ne25:2.4** Ye duggatīhi parimuccitukāmā bhavissanti, te dhammacārino bhavissantīti ayaṁ ettha bhagavato adhippāyo.

**ne25:2.5** Kokāliko hi sāriputtamoggallānesu theresu cittaṁ padosayitvā mahāpadumaniraye upapanno.

**ne25:2.6** Bhagavā ca satiārakkhena cetasā samannāgato, suttamhi vuttaṁ—

**ne25:2.7** “satiyā cittaṁ rakkhitabban”ti.

**ne25:2.8** Niyutto catubyūho hārasampāto.
