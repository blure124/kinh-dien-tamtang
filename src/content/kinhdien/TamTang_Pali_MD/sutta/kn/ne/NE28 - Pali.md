# Netti

# Paṭiniddesavāra

# Sampāta 9

# Parivattanahārasampāta

**ne28:1.1** Tattha katamo parivattano hārasampāto?

**ne28:2.1** “Tasmā rakkhitacittassa, sammāsaṅkappagocaro”ti gāthā.

**ne28:2.2** Samathavipassanāya bhāvitāya nirodho phalaṁ, pariññātaṁ dukkhaṁ, samudayo pahīno, maggo bhāvito paṭipakkhena.

**ne28:2.3** Niyutto parivattano hārasampāto.
