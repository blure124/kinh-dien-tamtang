# Netti

# Paṭiniddesavāra

# Sampāta 4

# Padaṭṭhānahārasampāta

**ne23:1.1** Tattha katamo padaṭṭhāno hārasampāto?

**ne23:2.1** “Tasmā rakkhitacittassa, sammāsaṅkappagocaro”ti gāthā.

**ne23:2.2** “Tasmā rakkhitacittassā”ti tiṇṇaṁ sucaritānaṁ padaṭṭhānaṁ.

**ne23:2.3** “Sammāsaṅkappagocaro”ti samathassa padaṭṭhānaṁ.

**ne23:2.4** “Sammādiṭṭhipurekkhāro”ti vipassanāya padaṭṭhānaṁ.

**ne23:2.5** “Ñatvāna udayabbayan”ti dassanabhūmiyā padaṭṭhānaṁ.

**ne23:2.6** “Thinamiddhābhibhū bhikkhū”ti vīriyassa padaṭṭhānaṁ.

**ne23:2.7** “Sabbā duggatiyo jahe”ti bhāvanāya padaṭṭhānaṁ.

**ne23:2.8** Niyutto padaṭṭhāno hārasampāto.
