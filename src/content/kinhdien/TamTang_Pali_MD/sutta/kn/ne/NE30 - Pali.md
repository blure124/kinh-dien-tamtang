# Netti

# Paṭiniddesavāra

# Sampāta 11

# Paññattihārasampāta

**ne30:1.1** Tattha katamo paññattihārasampāto?

**ne30:2.1** “Tasmā rakkhitacittassa, sammāsaṅkappagocaro”ti gāthā.

**ne30:2.2** “Tasmā rakkhitacittassā”ti padaṭṭhānapaññatti satiyā.

**ne30:2.3** “Sammāsaṅkappagocaro”ti bhāvanāpaññatti samathassa.

**ne30:2.4** “Sammādiṭṭhipurekkhāro, ñatvāna udayabbayan”ti dassanabhūmiyā nikkhepapaññatti.

**ne30:2.5** “Thinamiddhābhibhū bhikkhū”ti samudayassa anavasesappahānapaññatti, “sabbā duggatiyo jahe”ti bhāvanāpaññatti maggassa.

**ne30:2.6** Niyutto paññattihārasampāto.
