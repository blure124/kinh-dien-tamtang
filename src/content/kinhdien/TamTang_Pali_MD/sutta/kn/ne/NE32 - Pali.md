# Netti

# Paṭiniddesavāra

# Sampāta 13

# Sodhanahārasampāta

**ne32:1.1** Tattha katamo sodhano hārasampāto?

**ne32:2.1** “Tasmā rakkhitacittassa, sammāsaṅkappagocaro”ti gāthā.

**ne32:2.2** Yattha ārambho suddho, so pañho visajjito bhavati.

**ne32:2.3** Yattha pana ārambho na suddho, na tāva so pañho visajjito bhavati.

**ne32:2.4** Niyutto sodhano hārasampāto.
