# Therīgāthā 1.17

# Ekakanipāta

# Dhammātherīgāthā

**thig1.17:1.1** “Piṇḍapātaṁ caritvāna,

**thig1.17:1.2** daṇḍamolubbha dubbalā;

**thig1.17:1.3** Vedhamānehi gattehi,

**thig1.17:1.4** tattheva nipatiṁ chamā;

**thig1.17:1.5** Disvā ādīnavaṁ kāye,

**thig1.17:1.6** atha cittaṁ vimucci me”ti.

**thig1.17:2.1** … Dhammā therī ….
