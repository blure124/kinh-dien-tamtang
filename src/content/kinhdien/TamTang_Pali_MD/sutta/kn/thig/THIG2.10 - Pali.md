# Therīgāthā 2.10

# Dukanipāta

# Sāmātherīgāthā

**thig2.10:1.1** “Catukkhattuṁ pañcakkhattuṁ,

**thig2.10:1.2** vihārā upanikkhamiṁ;

**thig2.10:1.3** Aladdhā cetaso santiṁ,

**thig2.10:1.4** citte avasavattinī;

**thig2.10:1.5** Tassā me aṭṭhamī ratti,

**thig2.10:1.6** yato taṇhā samūhatā.

**thig2.10:2.1** Bahūhi dukkhadhammehi,

**thig2.10:2.2** appamādaratāya me;

**thig2.10:2.3** Taṇhakkhayo anuppatto,

**thig2.10:2.4** kataṁ buddhassa sāsanan”ti.

**thig2.10:3.1** … Sāmā therī ….

**thig2.10:4.1** Dukanipāto niṭṭhito.
