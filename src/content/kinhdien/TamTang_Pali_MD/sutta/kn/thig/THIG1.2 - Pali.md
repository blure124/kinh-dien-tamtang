# Therīgāthā 1.2

# Ekakanipāta

# Muttātherīgāthā

**thig1.2:1.1** “Mutte muccassu yogehi,

**thig1.2:1.2** cando rāhuggahā iva;

**thig1.2:1.3** Vippamuttena cittena,

**thig1.2:1.4** anaṇā bhuñja piṇḍakan”ti.

**thig1.2:2.1** Itthaṁ sudaṁ bhagavā muttaṁ sikkhamānaṁ imāya gāthāya abhiṇhaṁ ovadatīti.
