# Itthivagga

# 7. Ucchaṅgajātaka

**ja67:1.1** “Ucchaṅge deva me putto,

**ja67:1.2** Pathe dhāvantiyā pati;

**ja67:1.3** Tañca desaṁ na passāmi,

**ja67:1.4** Yato sodariyamānayeti.

**ja67:2.1** Ucchaṅgajātakaṁ sattamaṁ.
