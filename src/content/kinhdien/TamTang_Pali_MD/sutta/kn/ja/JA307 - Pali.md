# Catukkanipāta

# Kāliṅgavagga

# 7. Palāsajātaka

**ja307:1.1** “Acetanaṁ brāhmaṇa assuṇantaṁ,

**ja307:1.2** Jāno ajānantamimaṁ palāsaṁ;

**ja307:1.3** Āraddhaviriyo dhuvaṁ appamatto,

**ja307:1.4** Sukhaseyyaṁ pucchasi kissa hetu”.

**ja307:2.1** “Dūre suto ceva brahā ca rukkho,

**ja307:2.2** Dese ṭhito bhūtanivāsarūpo;

**ja307:2.3** Tasmā namassāmi imaṁ palāsaṁ,

**ja307:2.4** Ye cettha bhūtā te dhanassa hetu”.

**ja307:3.1** “So te karissāmi yathānubhāvaṁ,

**ja307:3.2** Kataññutaṁ brāhmaṇa pekkhamāno;

**ja307:3.3** Kathañhi āgamma sataṁ sakāse,

**ja307:3.4** Moghāni te assu pariphanditāni.

**ja307:4.1** Yo tindukarukkhassa paro pilakkho,

**ja307:4.2** Parivārito pubbayañño uḷāro;

**ja307:4.3** Tassesa mūlasmiṁ nidhi nikhāto,

**ja307:4.4** Adāyādo gaccha taṁ uddharāhī”ti.

**ja307:5.1** Palāsajātakaṁ sattamaṁ.
