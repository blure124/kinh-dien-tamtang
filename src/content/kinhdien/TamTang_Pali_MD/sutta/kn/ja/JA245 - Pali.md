# Siṅgālavagga

# 5. Mūlapariyāyajātaka

**ja245:1.1** “Kālo ghasati bhūtāni,

**ja245:1.2** sabbāneva sahattanā;

**ja245:1.3** Yo ca kālaghaso bhūto,

**ja245:1.4** sa bhūtapacaniṁ paci”.

**ja245:2.1** “Bahūni narasīsāni,

**ja245:2.2** lomasāni brahāni ca;

**ja245:2.3** Gīvāsu paṭimukkāni,

**ja245:2.4** kocidevettha kaṇṇavā”ti.

**ja245:3.1** Mūlapariyāyajātakaṁ pañcamaṁ.
