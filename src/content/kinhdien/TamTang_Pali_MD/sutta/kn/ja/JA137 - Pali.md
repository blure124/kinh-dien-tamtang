# Asampadānavagga

# 7. Babbujātaka

**ja137:1.1** “Yattheko labhate babbu,

**ja137:1.2** dutiyo tattha jāyati;

**ja137:1.3** Tatiyo ca catuttho ca,

**ja137:1.4** idaṁ te babbukā bilan”ti.

**ja137:2.1** Babbujātakaṁ sattamaṁ.
