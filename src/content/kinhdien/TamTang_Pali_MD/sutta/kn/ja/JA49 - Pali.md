# Atthakāmavagga

# 9. Nakkhattajātaka

**ja49:1.1** Nakkhattaṁ paṭimānentaṁ,

**ja49:1.2** attho bālaṁ upaccagā;

**ja49:1.3** Attho atthassa nakkhattaṁ,

**ja49:1.4** kiṁ karissanti tārakā”ti.

**ja49:2.1** Nakkhattajātakaṁ navamaṁ.
