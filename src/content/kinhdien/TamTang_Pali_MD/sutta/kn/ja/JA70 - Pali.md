# Itthivagga

# 10. Kuddālajātaka

**ja70:1.1** “Na taṁ jitaṁ sādhu jitaṁ,

**ja70:1.2** yaṁ jitaṁ avajīyati;

**ja70:1.3** Taṁ kho jitaṁ sādhu jitaṁ,

**ja70:1.4** yaṁ jitaṁ nāvajīyatī”ti.

**ja70:2.1** Kuddālajātakaṁ dasamaṁ.

**ja70:2.2** Itthivaggo sattamo.

**ja70:3.1** Tassuddānaṁ

**ja70:4.1** Sikhīsabbaghasopi ca vīṇavaro,

**ja70:4.2** Pisuṇā mittabhedikā nandī nadī;

**ja70:4.3** Mudulakkhaṇa sodariyā ca mano,

**ja70:4.4** Visa sādhujitena bhavanti dasāti.
