# Kusanāḷivagga

# 1. Kusanāḷijātaka

**ja121:1.1** “Kare sarikkho atha vāpi seṭṭho,

**ja121:1.2** Nihīnako vāpi kareyya eko;

**ja121:1.3** Kareyyumete byasane uttamatthaṁ,

**ja121:1.4** Yathā ahaṁ kusanāḷi rucāyan”ti.

**ja121:2.1** Kusanāḷijātakaṁ paṭhamaṁ.
