# Kakaṇṭakavagga

# 4. Naṅguṭṭhajātaka

**ja144:1.1** “Bahumpetaṁ asabbhi jātaveda,

**ja144:1.2** Yaṁ taṁ vāladhinābhipūjayāma;

**ja144:1.3** Maṁsārahassa natthajja maṁsaṁ,

**ja144:1.4** Naṅguṭṭhampi bhavaṁ paṭiggahātū”ti.

**ja144:2.1** Naṅguṭṭhajātakaṁ catutthaṁ.
