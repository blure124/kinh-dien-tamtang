# Jātaka

# Tikanipāta

# Kumbhavagga

# 1. Surāghaṭajātaka

**ja291:1.1** “Sabbakāmadadaṁ kumbhaṁ,

**ja291:1.2** kuṭaṁ laddhāna dhuttako;

**ja291:1.3** Yāva naṁ anupāleti,

**ja291:1.4** tāva so sukhamedhati.

**ja291:2.1** Yadā matto ca ditto ca,

**ja291:2.2** pamādā kumbhamabbhidā;

**ja291:2.3** Tadā naggo ca pottho ca,

**ja291:2.4** pacchā bālo vihaññati.

**ja291:3.1** Evameva yo dhanaṁ laddhā,

**ja291:3.2** Pamatto paribhuñjati;

**ja291:3.3** Pacchā tappati dummedho,

**ja291:3.4** Kuṭaṁ bhitvāva dhuttako”ti.

**ja291:4.1** Surāghaṭajātakaṁ paṭhamaṁ.
