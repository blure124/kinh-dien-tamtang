# Bīraṇathambhavagga

# 5. Kacchapajātaka

**ja215:1.1** “Avadhī vata attānaṁ,

**ja215:1.2** kacchapo byāharaṁ giraṁ;

**ja215:1.3** Suggahītasmiṁ kaṭṭhasmiṁ,

**ja215:1.4** vācāya sakiyāvadhi.

**ja215:2.1** Etampi disvā naravīriyaseṭṭha,

**ja215:2.2** Vācaṁ pamuñce kusalaṁ nātivelaṁ;

**ja215:2.3** Passasi bahubhāṇena,

**ja215:2.4** Kacchapaṁ byasanaṁ gatan”ti.

**ja215:3.1** Kacchapajātakaṁ pañcamaṁ.
