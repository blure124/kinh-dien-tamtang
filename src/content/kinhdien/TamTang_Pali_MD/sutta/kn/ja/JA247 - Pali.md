# Siṅgālavagga

# 7. Pādañjalījātaka

**ja247:1.1** “Addhā pādañjalī sabbe,

**ja247:1.2** paññāya atirocati;

**ja247:1.3** Tathā hi oṭṭhaṁ bhañjati,

**ja247:1.4** uttariṁ nūna passati”.

**ja247:2.1** “Nāyaṁ dhammaṁ adhammaṁ vā,

**ja247:2.2** Atthānatthañca bujjhati;

**ja247:2.3** Aññatra oṭṭhanibbhogā,

**ja247:2.4** Nāyaṁ jānāti kiñcanan”ti.

**ja247:3.1** Pādañjalījātakaṁ sattamaṁ.
