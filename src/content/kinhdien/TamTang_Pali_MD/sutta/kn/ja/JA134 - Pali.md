# Asampadānavagga

# 4. Jhānasodhanajātaka

**ja134:1.1** “Ye saññino tepi duggatā,

**ja134:1.2** Yepi asaññino tepi duggatā;

**ja134:1.3** Etaṁ ubhayaṁ vivajjaya,

**ja134:1.4** Taṁ samāpattisukhaṁ anaṅgaṇan”ti.

**ja134:2.1** Jhānasodhanajātakaṁ catutthaṁ.
