# Varuṇavagga

# 6. Asaṅkiyajātaka

**ja76:1.1** “Asaṅkiyomhi gāmamhi,

**ja76:1.2** araññe natthi me bhayaṁ;

**ja76:1.3** Ujuṁ maggaṁ samārūḷho,

**ja76:1.4** mettāya karuṇāya cā”ti.

**ja76:2.1** Asaṅkiyajātakaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.
