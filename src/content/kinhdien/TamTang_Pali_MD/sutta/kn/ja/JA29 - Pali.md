# Kuruṅgavagga

# 9. Kaṇhajātaka

**ja29:1.1** “Yato yato garu dhuraṁ,

**ja29:1.2** yato gambhīravattanī;

**ja29:1.3** Tadāssu kaṇhaṁ yuñjanti,

**ja29:1.4** svāssu taṁ vahate dhuran”ti.

**ja29:2.1** Kaṇhajātakaṁ navamaṁ.
