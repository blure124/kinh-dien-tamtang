# 5. Candābhajātaka

**ja135:1.1** “Candābhaṁ sūriyābhañca,

**ja135:1.2** yodha paññāya gādha”ti.

**ja135:1.3** Avitakkena jhānena,

**ja135:1.4** hoti ābhassarūpagoti.
