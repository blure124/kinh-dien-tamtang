# Atthakāmavagga

# 3. Veḷukajātaka

**ja43:1.1** “Yo atthakāmassa hitānukampino,

**ja43:1.2** Ovajjamāno na karoti sāsanaṁ;

**ja43:1.3** Evaṁ so nihato seti,

**ja43:1.4** Veḷukassa yathā pitā”ti.

**ja43:2.1** Veḷukajātakaṁ tatiyaṁ.
