# Atthakāmavagga

# 4. Makasajātaka

**ja44:1.1** “Seyyo amitto matiyā upeto,

**ja44:1.2** Na tveva mitto mativippahīno;

**ja44:1.3** Makasaṁ vadhissanti hi eḷamūgo,

**ja44:1.4** Putto pitu abbhidā uttamaṅgan”ti.

**ja44:2.1** Makasajātakaṁ catutthaṁ.
