# Asampadānavagga

# 2. Bhīrukajātaka

**ja132:1.1** “Kusalūpadese dhitiyā daḷhāya ca,

**ja132:1.2** Anivattitattābhayabhīrutāya ca;

**ja132:1.3** Na rakkhasīnaṁ vasamāgamimhase,

**ja132:1.4** Sa sotthibhāvo mahatā bhayena me”ti.

**ja132:2.1** Bhīrukajātakaṁ dutiyaṁ.
