# Apaṇṇakavagga

# 4. Cūḷaseṭṭhijātaka

**ja4:1.1** “Appakenapi medhāvī,

**ja4:1.2** pābhatena vicakkhaṇo;

**ja4:1.3** Samuṭṭhāpeti attānaṁ,

**ja4:1.4** aṇuṁ aggiṁva sandhaman”ti.

**ja4:2.1** Cūḷaseṭṭhijātakaṁ catutthaṁ.
