# Dukanipāta

# Kalyāṇavagga

# 1. Kalyāṇadhammajātaka

**ja171:1.1** “Kalyāṇadhammoti yadā janinda,

**ja171:1.2** Loke samaññaṁ anupāpuṇāti;

**ja171:1.3** Tasmā na hiyyetha naro sapañño,

**ja171:1.4** Hiriyāpi santo dhuramādiyanti.

**ja171:2.1** Sāyaṁ samaññā idha majja pattā,

**ja171:2.2** Kalyāṇadhammoti janinda loke;

**ja171:2.3** Tāhaṁ samekkhaṁ idha pabbajissaṁ,

**ja171:2.4** Na hi matthi chando idha kāmabhoge”ti.

**ja171:3.1** Kalyāṇadhammajātakaṁ paṭhamaṁ.
