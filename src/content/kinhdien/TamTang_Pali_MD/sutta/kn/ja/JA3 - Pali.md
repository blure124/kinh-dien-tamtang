# Apaṇṇakavagga

# 3. Serivavāṇijajātaka

**ja3:1.1** “Idha ce naṁ virādhesi,

**ja3:1.2** saddhammassa niyāmataṁ;

**ja3:1.3** Ciraṁ tvaṁ anutappesi,

**ja3:1.4** serivāyaṁva vāṇijo”ti.

**ja3:2.1** Serivavāṇijajātakaṁ tatiyaṁ.
