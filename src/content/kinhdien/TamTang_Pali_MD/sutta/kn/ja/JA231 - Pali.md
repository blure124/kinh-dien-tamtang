# Dukanipāta

# Upāhanavagga

# 1. Upāhanajātaka

**ja231:1.1** “Yathāpi kītā purisassupāhanā,

**ja231:1.2** Sukhassa atthāya dukhaṁ udabbahe;

**ja231:1.3** Ghammābhitattā thalasā papīḷitā,

**ja231:1.4** Tasseva pāde purisassa khādare.

**ja231:2.1** Evameva yo dukkulīno anariyo,

**ja231:2.2** Tammāka vijjañca sutañca ādiya;

**ja231:2.3** Tameva so tattha sutena khādati,

**ja231:2.4** Anariyo vuccati dupāhanūpamo”ti.

**ja231:3.1** Upāhanajātakaṁ paṭhamaṁ.
