# Parosatavagga

# 5. Dubbalakaṭṭhajātaka

**ja105:1.1** “Bahumpetaṁ vane kaṭṭhaṁ,

**ja105:1.2** vāto bhañjati dubbalaṁ;

**ja105:1.3** Tassa ce bhāyasī nāga,

**ja105:1.4** kiso nūna bhavissasī”ti.

**ja105:2.1** Dubbalakaṭṭhajātakaṁ pañcamaṁ.
