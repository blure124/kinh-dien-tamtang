# 6. Dubbacajātaka

**ja116:1.1** Atikaramakarācariya,

**ja116:1.2** Mayhampetaṁ na ruccati;

**ja116:1.3** Catutthe laṅghayitvāna,

**ja116:1.4** Pañcamāyasi āvuto”ti.

**ja116:2.1** Dubbacajātakaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.
