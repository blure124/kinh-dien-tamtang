# Ruhakavagga

# 3. Cūḷapadumajātaka

**ja193:1.1** “Ayameva sā ahamapi so anañño,

**ja193:1.2** Ayameva so hatthacchinno anañño;

**ja193:1.3** Yamāha ‘komārapatī maman’ti,

**ja193:1.4** Vajjhitthiyo natthi itthīsu saccaṁ.

**ja193:2.1** Imañca jammaṁ musalena hantvā,

**ja193:2.2** Luddaṁ chavaṁ paradārūpaseviṁ;

**ja193:2.3** Imissā ca naṁ pāpapatibbatāya,

**ja193:2.4** Jīvantiyā chindatha kaṇṇanāsan”ti.

**ja193:3.1** Cūḷapadumajātakaṁ tatiyaṁ.
