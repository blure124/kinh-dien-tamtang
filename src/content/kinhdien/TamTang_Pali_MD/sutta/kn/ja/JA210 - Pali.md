# Jātaka

# Dukanipāta

# Nataṁdaḷhavagga

# 10. Kandagalakajātaka

**ja210:1.1** “Ambho ko nāma yaṁ rukkho,

**ja210:1.2** sinnapatto sakaṇṭako;

**ja210:1.3** Yattha ekappahārena,

**ja210:1.4** uttamaṅgaṁ vibhijjitaṁ”.

**ja210:2.1** “Acāri vatāyaṁ vitudaṁ vanāni,

**ja210:2.2** Kaṭṭhaṅgarukkhesu asārakesu;

**ja210:2.3** Athāsadā khadiraṁ jātasāraṁ,

**ja210:2.4** Yatthabbhidā garuḷo uttamaṅgan”ti.

**ja210:3.1** Kandagalakajātakaṁ dasamaṁ.

**ja210:3.2** Nataṁdaḷhavaggo chaṭṭho.

**ja210:4.1** Tassuddānaṁ

**ja210:5.1** Daḷhabandhana haṁsavaro ca puna,

**ja210:5.2** Virūpakkha saviṭṭhaka macchavaro;

**ja210:5.3** Sakuruṅga saassaka ambavaro,

**ja210:5.4** Puna kukkuṭako garuḷena dasāti.
