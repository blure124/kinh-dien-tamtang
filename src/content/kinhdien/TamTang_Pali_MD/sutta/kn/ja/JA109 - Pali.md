# Parosatavagga

# 9. Kuṇḍapūvajātaka

**ja109:1.1** “Yathanno puriso hoti,

**ja109:1.2** tathannā tassa devatā;

**ja109:1.3** Āharetaṁ kuṇḍapūvaṁ,

**ja109:1.4** mā me bhāgaṁ vināsayā”ti.

**ja109:2.1** Kuṇḍapūvajātakaṁ navamaṁ.
