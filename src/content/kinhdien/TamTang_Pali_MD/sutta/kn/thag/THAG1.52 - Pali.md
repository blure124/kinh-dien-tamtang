# Theragāthā 1.52

# Ekakanipāta

# Chaṭṭhavagga

# Subāhuttheragāthā

**thag1.52:1.1** “Vassati devo yathā sugītaṁ,

**thag1.52:1.2** Channā me kuṭikā sukhā nivātā;

**thag1.52:1.3** Cittaṁ susamāhitañca kāye,

**thag1.52:1.4** Atha ce patthayasi pavassa devā”ti.

**thag1.52:1.5** … Subāhutthero ….
