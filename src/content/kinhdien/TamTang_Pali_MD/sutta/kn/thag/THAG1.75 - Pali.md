# Theragāthā 1.75

# Ekakanipāta

# Aṭṭhamavagga

# Susāradattheragāthā

**thag1.75:1.1** “Sādhu suvihitāna dassanaṁ,

**thag1.75:1.2** Kaṅkhā chijjati buddhi vaḍḍhati;

**thag1.75:1.3** Bālampi karonti paṇḍitaṁ,

**thag1.75:1.4** Tasmā sādhu sataṁ samāgamo”ti.

**thag1.75:1.5** … Susārado thero ….
