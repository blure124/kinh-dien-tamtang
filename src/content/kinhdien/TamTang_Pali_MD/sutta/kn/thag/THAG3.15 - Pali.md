# Theragāthā 3.15

# Tikanipāta

# Paṭhamavagga

# Hāritattheragāthā

**thag3.15:1.1** “Yo pubbe karaṇīyāni,

**thag3.15:1.2** pacchā so kātumicchati;

**thag3.15:1.3** Sukhā so dhaṁsate ṭhānā,

**thag3.15:1.4** pacchā ca manutappati.

**thag3.15:2.1** Yañhi kayirā tañhi vade,

**thag3.15:2.2** yaṁ na kayirā na taṁ vade;

**thag3.15:2.3** Akarontaṁ bhāsamānaṁ,

**thag3.15:2.4** parijānanti paṇḍitā.

**thag3.15:3.1** Susukhaṁ vata nibbānaṁ,

**thag3.15:3.2** sammāsambuddhadesitaṁ;

**thag3.15:3.3** Asokaṁ virajaṁ khemaṁ,

**thag3.15:3.4** yattha dukkhaṁ nirujjhatī”ti.

**thag3.15:3.5** … Hārito thero ….
