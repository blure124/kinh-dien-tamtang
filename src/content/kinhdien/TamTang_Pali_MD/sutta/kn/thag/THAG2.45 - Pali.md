# Theragāthā 2.45

# Dukanipāta

# Pañcamavagga

# Visākhapañcālaputtattheragāthā

**thag2.45:1.1** “Na ukkhipe no ca parikkhipe pare,

**thag2.45:1.2** Na okkhipe pāragataṁ na eraye;

**thag2.45:1.3** Na cattavaṇṇaṁ parisāsu byāhare,

**thag2.45:1.4** Anuddhato sammitabhāṇi subbato.

**thag2.45:2.1** Susukhumanipuṇatthadassinā,

**thag2.45:2.2** Matikusalena nivātavuttinā;

**thag2.45:2.3** Saṁsevitavuddhasīlinā,

**thag2.45:2.4** Nibbānaṁ na hi tena dullabhan”ti.

**thag2.45:2.5** … Visākho pañcālaputto thero ….
