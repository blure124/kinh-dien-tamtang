# Theragāthā 2.34

# Dukanipāta

# Catutthavagga

# Isidinnattheragāthā

**thag2.34:1.1** “Diṭṭhā mayā dhammadharā upāsakā,

**thag2.34:1.2** Kāmā aniccā iti bhāsamānā;

**thag2.34:1.3** Sārattarattā maṇikuṇḍalesu,

**thag2.34:1.4** Puttesu dāresu ca te apekkhā.

**thag2.34:2.1** Addhā na jānanti yatodha dhammaṁ,

**thag2.34:2.2** Kāmā aniccā iti cāpi āhu;

**thag2.34:2.3** Rāgañca tesaṁ na balatthi chettuṁ,

**thag2.34:2.4** Tasmā sitā puttadāraṁ dhanañcā”ti.

**thag2.34:2.5** … Isidinno thero ….
