# Theragāthā 2.42

# Dukanipāta

# Pañcamavagga

# Dhammapālattheragāthā

**thag2.42:1.1** “Yo have daharo bhikkhu,

**thag2.42:1.2** yuñjati buddhasāsane;

**thag2.42:1.3** Jāgaro sa hi suttesu,

**thag2.42:1.4** amoghaṁ tassa jīvitaṁ.

**thag2.42:2.1** Tasmā saddhañca sīlañca,

**thag2.42:2.2** pasādaṁ dhammadassanaṁ;

**thag2.42:2.3** Anuyuñjetha medhāvī,

**thag2.42:2.4** saraṁ buddhāna sāsanan”ti.

**thag2.42:2.5** … Dhammapālo thero ….
