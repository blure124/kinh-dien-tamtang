# Aṅguttara Nikāya 10.132

# 13. Parisuddhavagga

# Dasamasutta

**an10.132:1.1** “Dasayime, bhikkhave, micchattā.

**an10.132:1.2** Katame dasa?

**an10.132:1.3** Micchādiṭṭhi, micchāsaṅkappo, micchāvācā, micchākammanto, micchāājīvo, micchāvāyāmo, micchāsati, micchāsamādhi, micchāñāṇaṁ, micchāvimutti—

**an10.132:1.4** ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa micchattā”ti.

**an10.132:1.5** Dasamaṁ.
