# Aṅguttara Nikāya 10.135

# 14. Sādhuvagga

# Ariyadhammasutta

**an10.135:1.1** “Ariyadhammañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi anariyadhammañca.

**an10.135:1.2** Taṁ suṇātha …pe…

**an10.135:1.3** katamo ca, bhikkhave, anariyo dhammo?

**an10.135:1.4** Micchādiṭṭhi …pe… micchāvimutti—

**an10.135:1.5** ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, anariyo dhammo.

**an10.135:1.6** Katamo ca, bhikkhave, ariyo dhammo?

**an10.135:1.7** Sammādiṭṭhi …pe… sammāvimutti—

**an10.135:1.8** ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, ariyo dhammo”ti.

**an10.135:1.9** Dutiyaṁ.
