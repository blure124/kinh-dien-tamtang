# Aṅguttara Nikāya 10.125

# 13. Parisuddhavagga

# Tatiyasutta

**an10.125:1.1** “Dasayime, bhikkhave, dhammā mahapphalā mahānisaṁsā, nāññatra sugatavinayā.

**an10.125:1.2** Katame dasa?

**an10.125:1.3** Sammādiṭṭhi …pe… sammāvimutti—

**an10.125:1.4** ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa dhammā mahapphalā mahānisaṁsā, nāññatra sugatavinayā”ti.

**an10.125:1.5** Tatiyaṁ.
