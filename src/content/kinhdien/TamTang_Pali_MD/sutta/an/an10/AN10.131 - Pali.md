# Aṅguttara Nikāya 10.131

# 13. Parisuddhavagga

# Navamasutta

**an10.131:1.1** “Dasayime, bhikkhave, dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā ekantanibbidāya virāgāya nirodhāya upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā.

**an10.131:1.2** Katame dasa?

**an10.131:1.3** Sammādiṭṭhi …pe… sammāvimutti—

**an10.131:1.4** ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā ekantanibbidāya virāgāya nirodhāya upasamāya abhiññāya sambodhāya nibbānāya saṁvattanti, nāññatra sugatavinayā”ti.

**an10.131:1.5** Navamaṁ.
