# Aṅguttara Nikāya 10.129

# 13. Parisuddhavagga

# Sattamasutta

**an10.129:1.1** “Dasayime, bhikkhave, dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā mahapphalā honti mahānisaṁsā, nāññatra sugatavinayā.

**an10.129:1.2** Katame dasa?

**an10.129:1.3** Sammādiṭṭhi …pe… sammāvimutti—

**an10.129:1.4** ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa dhammā bhāvitā bahulīkatā mahapphalā honti mahānisaṁsā, nāññatra sugatavinayā”ti.

**an10.129:1.5** Sattamaṁ.
