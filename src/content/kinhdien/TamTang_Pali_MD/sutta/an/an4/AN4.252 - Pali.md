# Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.252

# 25. Āpattibhayavagga

# Tatiyavohārasutta

**an4.252:1.1** “Cattārome, bhikkhave, anariyavohārā.

**an4.252:1.2** Katame cattāro?

**an4.252:1.3** Diṭṭhe adiṭṭhavāditā, sute asutavāditā, mute amutavāditā, viññāte aviññātavāditā—

**an4.252:1.4** Ime kho, bhikkhave, cattāro anariyavohārā”ti.

**an4.252:1.5** Dasamaṁ.
