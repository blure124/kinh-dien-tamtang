# Aṅguttara Nikāya 4.72

# 8. Apaṇṇakavagga

# Sammādiṭṭhisutta

**an4.72:1.1** “Catūhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu apaṇṇakappaṭipadaṁ paṭipanno hoti, yoni cassa āraddhā hoti āsavānaṁ khayāya.

**an4.72:1.2** Katamehi catūhi?

**an4.72:1.3** Nekkhammavitakkena, abyāpādavitakkena, avihiṁsāvitakkena, sammādiṭṭhiyā—

**an4.72:1.4** imehi kho, bhikkhave, catūhi dhammehi samannāgato bhikkhu apaṇṇakappaṭipadaṁ paṭipanno hoti, yoni cassa āraddhā hoti āsavānaṁ khayāyā”ti.

**an4.72:1.5** Dutiyaṁ.
