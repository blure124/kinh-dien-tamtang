# Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.183

# 19. Araññavagga

# Rukkhamūlikasutta

**an5.183:1.1** “Pañcime, bhikkhave, rukkhamūlikā.

**an5.183:1.2** Katame pañca?

**an5.183:1.3** Mandattā momūhattā rukkhamūliko hoti …pe… idamatthitaṁyeva nissāya rukkhamūliko hoti.

**an5.183:1.4** Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca rukkhamūlikā”ti.

**an5.183:1.5** Tatiyaṁ.
