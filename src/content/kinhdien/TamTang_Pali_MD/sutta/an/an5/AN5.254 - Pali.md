# Aṅguttara Nikāya 5.254

# 26. Upasampadāvagga

# Pañcamacchariyasutta

**an5.254:1.1** “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, macchariyāni.

**an5.254:1.2** Katamāni pañca?

**an5.254:1.3** Āvāsamacchariyaṁ, kulamacchariyaṁ, lābhamacchariyaṁ, vaṇṇamacchariyaṁ, dhammamacchariyaṁ—

**an5.254:1.4** imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañca macchariyāni.

**an5.254:1.5** Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ macchariyānaṁ etaṁ paṭikuṭṭhaṁ, yadidaṁ dhammamacchariyan”ti.

**an5.254:1.6** Catutthaṁ.
