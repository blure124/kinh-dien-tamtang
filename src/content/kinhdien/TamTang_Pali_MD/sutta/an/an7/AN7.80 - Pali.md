# Aṅguttara Nikāya 7.80

# 8. Vinayavagga

# Dutiyavinayadharasobhanasutta

**an7.80:1.1** “Sattahi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato vinayadharo sobhati.

**an7.80:1.2** Katamehi sattahi?

**an7.80:1.3** Āpattiṁ jānāti,

**an7.80:1.4** anāpattiṁ jānāti,

**an7.80:1.5** lahukaṁ āpattiṁ jānāti,

**an7.80:1.6** garukaṁ āpattiṁ jānāti,

**an7.80:1.7** ubhayāni kho panassa pātimokkhāni vitthārena svāgatāni honti suvibhattāni suppavattīni suvinicchitāni suttaso anubyañjanaso,

**an7.80:1.8** catunnaṁ jhānānaṁ …pe… akasiralābhī,

**an7.80:1.9** āsavānaṁ khayā …pe… sacchikatvā upasampajja viharati.

**an7.80:1.10** Imehi kho, bhikkhave, sattahi dhammehi samannāgato vinayadharo sobhatī”ti.

**an7.80:1.11** Chaṭṭhaṁ.
