# Aṅguttara Nikāya 7.17

# 2. Anusayavagga

# Dukkhānupassīsutta

**an7.17:1.1** Sattime, bhikkhave, puggalā āhuneyyā …pe… anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassa.

**an7.17:1.2** Katame satta?

**an7.17:1.3** Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco puggalo sabbasaṅkhāresu dukkhānupassī viharati …pe….

**an7.17:1.4** Sattamaṁ.
