# Aṅguttara Nikāya 3.113

# 12. Āpāyikavagga

# Āpāyikasutta

**an3.113:1.1** “Tayome, bhikkhave, āpāyikā nerayikā idamappahāya.

**an3.113:1.2** Katame tayo?

**an3.113:1.3** Yo ca abrahmacārī brahmacāripaṭiñño,

**an3.113:1.4** yo ca suddhaṁ brahmacariyaṁ carantaṁ amūlakena abrahmacariyena anuddhaṁseti,

**an3.113:1.5** yo cāyaṁ evaṁvādī evaṁdiṭṭhi: ‘natthi kāmesu doso’ti, so tāya kāmesu pātabyataṁ āpajjati.

**an3.113:1.6** Ime kho, bhikkhave, tayo āpāyikā nerayikā idamappahāyā”ti.

**an3.113:1.7** Paṭhamaṁ.
