# Aṅguttara Nikāya 3.114

# 12. Āpāyikavagga

# Dullabhasutta

**an3.114:1.1** “Tiṇṇaṁ, bhikkhave, pātubhāvo dullabho lokasmiṁ.

**an3.114:1.2** Katamesaṁ tiṇṇaṁ?

**an3.114:1.3** Tathāgatassa, bhikkhave, arahato sammāsambuddhassa pātubhāvo dullabho lokasmiṁ, tathāgatappaveditassa dhammavinayassa desetā puggalo dullabho lokasmiṁ, kataññū katavedī puggalo dullabho lokasmiṁ.

**an3.114:1.4** Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, tiṇṇaṁ pātubhāvo dullabho lokasmin”ti.

**an3.114:1.5** Dutiyaṁ.
