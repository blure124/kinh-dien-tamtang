# Aṅguttara Nikāya 9

# 10. Rāgapeyyāla

# ~

**an9.93:1.1** “Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya nava dhammā bhāvetabbā.

**an9.93:1.2** Katame nava?

**an9.93:1.3** Asubhasaññā, maraṇasaññā, āhāre paṭikūlasaññā, sabbaloke anabhiratasaññā, aniccasaññā, anicce dukkhasaññā, dukkhe anattasaññā, pahānasaññā, virāgasaññā—

**an9.93:1.4** rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya ime nava dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti.
