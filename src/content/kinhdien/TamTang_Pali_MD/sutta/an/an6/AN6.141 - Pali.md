# Aṅguttara Nikāya 6

# 13. Rāgapeyyāla

# ~

**an6.141:1.1** “Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya cha dhammā bhāvetabbā.

**an6.141:1.2** Katame cha?

**an6.141:1.3** Buddhānussati, dhammānussati, saṅghānussati, sīlānussati, cāgānussati, devatānussati.

**an6.141:1.4** Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya ime cha dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti.
