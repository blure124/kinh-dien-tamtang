# Aṅguttara Nikāya 6

# 13. Rāgapeyyāla

# ~

**an6.142:1.1** “Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya cha dhammā bhāvetabbā.

**an6.142:1.2** Katame cha?

**an6.142:1.3** Aniccasaññā, anicce dukkhasaññā, dukkhe anattasaññā, pahānasaññā, virāgasaññā, nirodhasaññā.

**an6.142:1.4** Rāgassa, bhikkhave, abhiññāya ime cha dhammā bhāvetabbā”ti.
