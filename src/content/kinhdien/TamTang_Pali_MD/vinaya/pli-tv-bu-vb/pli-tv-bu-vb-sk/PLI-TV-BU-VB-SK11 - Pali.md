# Ujjagghikavagga

# 11. Ujjagghikasikkhāpada

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.1** Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.2** Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū mahāhasitaṁ hasantā antaraghare gacchanti …pe….

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.3.1** “Na ujjagghikāya antaraghare gamissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.4** Na ujjagghikāya antaraghare gantabbaṁ.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.5** Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca mahāhasitaṁ hasanto antaraghare gacchati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.6.1** Anāpatti—

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.7** asañcicca,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.8** assatiyā,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.9** ajānantassa,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.10** gilānassa,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.11** hasanīyasmiṁ vatthusmiṁ mihitamattaṁ karoti,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.12** āpadāsu,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.13** ummattakassa,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.14** ādikammikassāti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk11:1.15** Paṭhamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
