# Surusuruvagga

# 54. Oṭṭhanillehakasikkhāpada

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk54:1.1** Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk54:1.2** Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū oṭṭhanillehakaṁ bhuñjanti …pe…

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk54:1.3.1** “Na oṭṭhanillehakaṁ bhuñjissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk54:1.4** Na oṭṭhanillehakaṁ bhuñjitabbaṁ.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk54:1.5** Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca oṭṭhanillehakaṁ bhuñjati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk54:1.6.1** Anāpatti—

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk54:1.7.1** asañcicca …pe…

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk54:1.8** ādikammikassāti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk54:1.9** Catutthasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
