# Kabaḷavagga

# 43. Sakabaḷasikkhāpada

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk43:1.1** Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk43:1.2** Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū sakabaḷena mukhena byāharanti …pe….

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk43:1.3.1** “Na sakabaḷena mukhena byāharissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk43:1.4** Na sakabaḷena mukhena byāharitabbaṁ.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk43:1.5** Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca sakabaḷena mukhena byāharati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk43:1.6.1** Anāpatti—

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk43:1.7.1** asañcicca …pe…

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk43:1.8** ādikammikassāti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk43:1.9** Tatiyasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
