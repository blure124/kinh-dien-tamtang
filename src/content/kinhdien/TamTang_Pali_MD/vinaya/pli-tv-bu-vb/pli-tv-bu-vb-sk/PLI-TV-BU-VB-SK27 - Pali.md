# Khambhakatavagga

# 27. Sakkaccapaṭiggahaṇasikkhāpada

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk27:1.1** Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk27:1.2** Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū asakkaccaṁ piṇḍapātaṁ paṭiggaṇhanti chaḍḍetukāmā viya …pe….

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk27:1.3.1** “Sakkaccaṁ piṇḍapātaṁ paṭiggahessāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk27:1.4** Sakkaccaṁ piṇḍapāto paṭiggahetabbo.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk27:1.5** Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca asakkaccaṁ piṇḍapātaṁ paṭiggaṇhāti chaḍḍetukāmo viya, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk27:1.6.1** Anāpatti—

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk27:1.7.1** asañcicca …pe…

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk27:1.8** ādikammikassāti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk27:1.9** Sattamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
