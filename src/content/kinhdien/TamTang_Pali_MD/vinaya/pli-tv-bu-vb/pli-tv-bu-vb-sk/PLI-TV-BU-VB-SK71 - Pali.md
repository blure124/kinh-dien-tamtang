# Pādukavagga

# 71. Pacchatogamanasikkhāpada

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk71:1.1** Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk71:1.2** Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū pacchato gacchantā purato gacchantassa dhammaṁ desenti …pe….

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk71:1.3.1** “Na pacchato gacchanto purato gacchantassa agilānassa dhammaṁ desessāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk71:1.4** Na pacchato gacchantena purato gacchantassa agilānassa dhammo desetabbo.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk71:1.5** Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca pacchato gacchanto purato gacchantassa agilānassa dhammaṁ deseti, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk71:1.6.1** Anāpatti—

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk71:1.7.1** asañcicca …pe…

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk71:1.8** ādikammikassāti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk71:1.9** Ekādasamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
