# Surusuruvagga

# 58. Daṇḍapāṇisikkhāpada

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk58:1.1** Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk58:1.2** Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū daṇḍapāṇissa dhammaṁ desenti …pe….

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk58:1.3.1** “Na daṇḍapāṇissa agilānassa dhammaṁ desessāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk58:1.4.1** Daṇḍo nāma

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk58:1.5** majjhimassa purisassa catuhattho daṇḍo.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk58:1.6** Tato ukkaṭṭho adaṇḍo, omako adaṇḍo.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk58:1.7** Na daṇḍapāṇissa agilānassa dhammo desetabbo.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk58:1.8** Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca daṇḍapāṇissa agilānassa dhammaṁ deseti, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk58:1.9.1** Anāpatti—

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk58:1.10.1** asañcicca …pe…

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk58:1.11** ādikammikassāti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk58:1.12** Aṭṭhamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
