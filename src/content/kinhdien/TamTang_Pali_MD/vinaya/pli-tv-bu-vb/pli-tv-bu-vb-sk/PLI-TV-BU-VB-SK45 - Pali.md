# Kabaḷavagga

# 45. Kabaḷāvacchedakasikkhāpada

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.1** Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.2** Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū kabaḷāvacchedakaṁ bhuñjanti …pe….

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.3.1** “Na kabaḷāvacchedakaṁ bhuñjissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.4** Na kabaḷāvacchedakaṁ bhuñjitabbaṁ.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.5** Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca kabaḷāvacchedakaṁ bhuñjati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.6.1** Anāpatti—

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.7** asañcicca,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.8** assatiyā,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.9** ajānantassa,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.10** gilānassa,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.11** khajjake,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.12** phalāphale,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.13** uttaribhaṅge,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.14** āpadāsu,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.15** ummattakassa,

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.16** ādikammikassāti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk45:1.17** Pañcamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
