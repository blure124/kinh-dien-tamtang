# Surusuruvagga

# 52. Hatthanillehakasikkhāpada

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk52:1.1** Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk52:1.2** Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū hatthanillehakaṁ bhuñjanti …pe….

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk52:1.3.1** “Na hatthanillehakaṁ bhuñjissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk52:1.4** Na hatthanillehakaṁ bhuñjitabbaṁ.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk52:1.5** Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca hatthanillehakaṁ bhuñjati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk52:1.6.1** Anāpatti—

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk52:1.7.1** asañcicca …pe…

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk52:1.8** ādikammikassāti.

**pli-tv-bu-vb-sk52:1.9** Dutiyasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
