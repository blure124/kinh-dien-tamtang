# Parivāra

# Bhikkhuvibhaṅga

# Dutiyabhāga

# 1.12 Saṅgahitavāra

**pli-tv-pvr1.12:1.1** Methunaṁ dhammaṁ paṭisevanapaccayā āpattiyo sattannaṁ āpattikkhandhānaṁ katihi āpattikkhandhehi saṅgahitā?

**pli-tv-pvr1.12:1.2** Methunaṁ dhammaṁ paṭisevanapaccayā āpattiyo sattannaṁ āpattikkhandhānaṁ catūhi āpattikkhandhehi saṅgahitā—

**pli-tv-pvr1.12:1.3** siyā pārājikāpattikkhandhena, siyā thullaccayāpattikkhandhena, siyā pācittiyāpattikkhandhena, siyā dukkaṭāpattikkhandhena …pe….

**pli-tv-pvr1.12:2.1** Anādariyaṁ paṭicca udake uccāraṁ vā passāvaṁ vā kheḷaṁ vā karaṇapaccayā āpatti sattannaṁ āpattikkhandhānaṁ katihi āpattikkhandhehi saṅgahitā?

**pli-tv-pvr1.12:2.2** Anādariyaṁ paṭicca udake uccāraṁ vā passāvaṁ vā kheḷaṁ vā karaṇapaccayā āpatti sattannaṁ āpattikkhandhānaṁ ekena āpattikkhandhena saṅgahitā—

**pli-tv-pvr1.12:2.3** dukkaṭāpattikkhandhena.

**pli-tv-pvr1.12:3.1** Saṅgahitavāro niṭṭhito catuttho.
