# 8.43. Cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhūduka

**patthana8.43:1.1** Nacittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nacittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhū dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā …. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana8.43:2.1** Hetuyā nava …pe… avigate nava.
