# Dhammapaccanīya (2), Dukapaṭṭhānapāḷi (2)

# 8.40. Cittasahabhūduka

**patthana8.40:1.1** Nacittasahabhuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nacittasahabhū dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā …. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana8.40:2.1** Hetuyā nava …pe… purejāte āsevane pañca …pe… avigate nava.
