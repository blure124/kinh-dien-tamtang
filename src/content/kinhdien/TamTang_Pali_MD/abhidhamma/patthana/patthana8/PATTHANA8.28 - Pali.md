# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammapaccanīya (2), Dukapaṭṭhānapāḷi (2)

# 8.28. Ganthasampayuttaduka

**patthana8.28:1.1** Naganthasampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naganthasampayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Naganthasampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naganthavippayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Naganthasampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naganthasampayutto ca naganthavippayutto ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā.

**patthana8.28:2.1** Naganthavippayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naganthavippayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Naganthavippayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naganthasampayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Naganthavippayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naganthasampayutto ca naganthavippayutto ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā.

**patthana8.28:3.1** Naganthasampayuttañca naganthavippayuttañca dhammaṁ paṭicca naganthasampayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Naganthasampayuttañca naganthavippayuttañca dhammaṁ paṭicca naganthavippayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Naganthasampayuttañca naganthavippayuttañca dhammaṁ paṭicca naganthasampayutto ca naganthavippayutto ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā.  (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana8.28:4.1** Hetuyā nava, ārammaṇe cha, adhipatiyā nava …pe… vipāke ekaṁ …pe… avigate nava. (Sabbattha vitthāro.)
