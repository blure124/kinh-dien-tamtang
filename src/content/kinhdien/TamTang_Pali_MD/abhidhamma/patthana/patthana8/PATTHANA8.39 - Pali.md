# 8.39. Cittasamuṭṭhānaduka

**patthana8.39:1.1** Nacittasamuṭṭhānaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nacittasamuṭṭhāno dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā …. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana8.39:2.1** Hetuyā nava …pe… adhipatiyā pañca …pe… purejāte āsevane pañca …pe… avigate nava.
