# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Tikatikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (5)

# 17.22. Vipākattika, Kusalattika

**patthana17.22:1.1** Vipākadhammadhammaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca navipākadhammadhammo nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi …pe… avigate tīṇi.

**patthana17.22:2.1** Vipākadhammadhammaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca navipākadhammadhammo naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi …pe… avigate tīṇi.

**patthana17.22:3.1** Nevavipākanavipākadhammadhammaṁ abyākataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā nanevavipākanavipākadhammadhammo naabyākato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi …pe… avigate tīṇi.
