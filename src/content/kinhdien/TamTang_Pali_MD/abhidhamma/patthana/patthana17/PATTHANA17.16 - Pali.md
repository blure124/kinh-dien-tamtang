# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Tikatikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (5)

# 17.16. Kusalattika, Uppannattika

**patthana17.16:1.1** Kusalo anuppanno dhammo nakusalassa naanuppannassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana17.16:2.1** Ārammaṇe aṭṭhārasa, adhipatiyā tīṇi, upanissaye aṭṭhārasa. (Uppādī anuppannasadisaṁ.)
