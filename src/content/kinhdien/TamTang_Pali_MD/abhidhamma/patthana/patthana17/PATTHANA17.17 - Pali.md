# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Tikatikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (5)

# 17.17. Kusalattika, Atītattika

**patthana17.17:1.1** Kusalo atīto dhammo nakusalassa naatītassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana17.17:2.1** Ārammaṇe aṭṭhārasa, adhipatiyā ekādasa, anantare samanantare soḷasa, upanissaye aṭṭhārasa, natthiyā vigate soḷasa. (Anāgataṁ atītasadisaṁ.)
