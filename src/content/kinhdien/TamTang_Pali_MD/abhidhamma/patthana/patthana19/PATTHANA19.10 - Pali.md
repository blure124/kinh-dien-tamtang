# 19.10. Ācayagāmittika

**patthana19.10:1.1** Naācayagāmiṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca apacayagāmī dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana19.10:2.1** Hetuyā aṭṭhārasa, ārammaṇe nava …pe… vipāke tīṇi …pe… avigate aṭṭhārasa.
