# Dhammapaccanīyānuloma (4), Tikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (1)

# 19.9. Dassanahetuttika

**patthana19.9:1.1** Nadassanena pahātabbahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca dassanena pahātabbahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana19.9:2.1** Hetuyā chabbīsa, ārammaṇe ekavīsa, adhipatiyā aṭṭhārasa …pe… vipāke tīṇi …pe… avigate chabbīsa.
