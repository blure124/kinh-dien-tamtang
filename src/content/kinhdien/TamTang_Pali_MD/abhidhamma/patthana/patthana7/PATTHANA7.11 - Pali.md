# Dhammapaccanīya (2), Tikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (1)

# 7.11. Sekkhattika

**patthana7.11:1.1** Nasekkhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …pe… naasekkhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …pe… nanevasekkhanāsekkhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana7.11:2.1** Hetuyā ekūnatiṁsa, ārammaṇe catuvīsa …pe… āsevane sattarasa …pe… avigate ekūnatiṁsa.
