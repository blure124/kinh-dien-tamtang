# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammapaccanīya (2), Tikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (1)

# 7.8. Dassanattika

**patthana7.8:1.1** Nadassanena pahātabbaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …pe… nabhāvanāya pahātabbaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …pe… nanevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana7.8:2.1** Hetuyā ekūnatiṁsa, ārammaṇe catuvīsa …pe… vipāke nava …pe… avigate ekūnatiṁsa.
