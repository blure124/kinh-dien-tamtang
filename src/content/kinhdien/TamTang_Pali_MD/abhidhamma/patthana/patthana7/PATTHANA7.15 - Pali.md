# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammapaccanīya (2), Tikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (1)

# 7.15. Micchattattika

**patthana7.15:1.1** Namicchattaniyataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …pe… nasammattaniyataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …pe… naaniyataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana7.15:2.1** Hetuyā ekūnatiṁsa, ārammaṇe catuvīsa …pe… vipāke nava …pe… avigate ekūnatiṁsa.
