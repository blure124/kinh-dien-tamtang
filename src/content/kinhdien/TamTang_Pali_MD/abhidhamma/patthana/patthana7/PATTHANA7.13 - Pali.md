# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammapaccanīya (2), Tikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (1)

# 7.13. Parittārammaṇattika

**patthana7.13:1.1** Naparittārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …pe… namahaggatārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …pe… naappamāṇārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana7.13:2.1** Hetuyā ekūnapaññāsa …pe… avigate ekūnapaññāsa.
