# Dhammapaccanīya (2), Tikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (1)

# 7.16. Maggārammaṇattika

**patthana7.16:1.1** Namaggārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …pe… namaggahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …pe… namaggādhipatiṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana7.16:2.1** Hetuyā ekūnapaññāsa …pe… avigate ekūnapaññāsa.
