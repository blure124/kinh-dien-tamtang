# 7.20. Ajjhattattika

**patthana7.20:1.1** Naajjhattaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naajjhatto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā.

**patthana7.20:2.1** Nabahiddhā dhammaṁ paṭicca nabahiddhā dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)
