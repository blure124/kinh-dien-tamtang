# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammapaccanīya (2), Tikadukapaṭṭhānapāḷi (4)

# 10.16. Sanidassanattika, Upādānadukādi

**patthana10.16:1.1** Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ noupādānaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …pe… nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ nokilesaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana10.16:2.1** Hetuyā tiṁsa, ārammaṇe nava …pe… aññamaññe pañcavīsa …pe… avigate tiṁsa.
