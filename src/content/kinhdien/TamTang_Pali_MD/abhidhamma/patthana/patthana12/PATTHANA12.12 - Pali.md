# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammapaccanīya (2), Dukadukapaṭṭhānapāḷi (6)

# 12.12. Upādānadukādi, Hetuduka

**patthana12.12:1.1** Noupādānaṁ nahetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana12.12:2.1** Hetuyā pañca, vipāke ekaṁ …pe… avigate pañca.

**patthana12.12:3.1** Nokilesaṁ nahetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …pe… nanokilesaṁ nahetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca …. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana12.12:4.1** Hetuyā nava …pe… vipāke ekaṁ …pe… avigate nava.
