# 21.54. Kilesadukādi, Sanidassanattika

**patthana21.54:1.1** Nokilesaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nokileso sanidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana21.54:2.1** Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi …pe… vipāke ekaṁ …pe… avigate tīṇi.
