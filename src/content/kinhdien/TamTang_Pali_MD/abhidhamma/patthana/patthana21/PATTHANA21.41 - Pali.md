# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammapaccanīyānuloma (4), Dukatikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (3)

# 21.41. Sanidassanaduka, Sanidassanattika

**patthana21.41:1.1** Nasanidassanaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sanidassano sanidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā ekaṁ, adhipatiyā ekaṁ …pe… avigate ekaṁ.

**patthana21.41:2.1** Nasanidassanaṁ naanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca anidassano anidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā ekaṁ, adhipatiyā ekaṁ …pe… avigate ekaṁ.

**patthana21.41:3.1** Nasanidassanaṁ naanidassanaappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca anidassano anidassanaappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā ekaṁ, adhipatiyā ekaṁ …pe… avigate ekaṁ.
