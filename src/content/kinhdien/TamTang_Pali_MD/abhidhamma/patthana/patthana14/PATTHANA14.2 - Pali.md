# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Dukapaṭṭhānapāḷi (2)

# 14.2. Sahetukaduka

**patthana14.2:1.1** Sahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Sahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Sahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasahetuko ca naahetuko ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā.

**patthana14.2:2.1** Ahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Ahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Ahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasahetuko ca naahetuko ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā.

**patthana14.2:3.1** Sahetukañca ahetukañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nasahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Sahetukañca ahetukañca dhammaṁ paṭicca naahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Sahetukañca ahetukañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nasahetuko ca naahetuko ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā.  (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana14.2:4.1** Hetuyā nava, ārammaṇe cha, adhipatiyā pañca …pe… avigate nava. (Sahajātavārampi …pe… pañhāvārampi vitthāretabbaṁ.)
