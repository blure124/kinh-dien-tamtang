# Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Dukapaṭṭhānapāḷi (2)

# 14.24. Upādānagocchaka

**patthana14.24:1.1** Upādānaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca noupādāno dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana14.24:2.1** Hetuyā nava …pe… vipāke ekaṁ …pe… avigate nava.
