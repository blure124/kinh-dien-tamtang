# Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Dukapaṭṭhānapāḷi (2)

# 14.25. Kilesagocchaka

**patthana14.25:1.1** Kilesaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nokileso dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana14.25:2.1** Hetuyā nava …pe… vipāke ekaṁ …pe… avigate nava.
