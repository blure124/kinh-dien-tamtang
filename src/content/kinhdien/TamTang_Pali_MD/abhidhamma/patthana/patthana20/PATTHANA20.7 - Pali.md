# 20.7. Kilesagocchaka

**patthana20.7:1.1** Nakilesaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca kileso dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana20.7:2.1** Hetuyā nava …pe… vipāke ekaṁ …pe… avigate nava. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)
