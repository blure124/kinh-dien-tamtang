# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammapaccanīya (2), Dukatikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (3)

# 9.2. Hetuduka, Vedanāttika

**patthana9.2:1.1** Nahetuṁ nasukhāya vedanāya sampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu nasukhāya vedanāya sampayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Nahetuṁ nasukhāya vedanāya sampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nanahetu nasukhāya vedanāya sampayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana9.2:2.1** Hetuyā nava …pe… avigate nava. (Sabbattha nava.)

**patthana9.2:3.1** Nahetuṁ nadukkhāya vedanāya sampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu nadukkhāya vedanāya sampayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana9.2:4.1** Hetuyā nava …pe… avigate nava. (Sabbattha nava.)

**patthana9.2:5.1** Nahetuṁ naadukkhamasukhāya vedanāya sampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu naadukkhamasukhāya vedanāya sampayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana9.2:6.1** Hetuyā nava …pe… avigate nava. (Sabbattha nava.)
