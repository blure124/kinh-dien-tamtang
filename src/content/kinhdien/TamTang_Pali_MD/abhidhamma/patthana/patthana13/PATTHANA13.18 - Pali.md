# Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Tikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (1)

# 13.18. Atītattika

**patthana13.18:1.1** Paccuppanno dhammo naatītassa dhammassa hetupaccayena paccayo. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana13.18:2.1** Hetuyā tīṇi, ārammaṇe nava, adhipatiyā nava …pe… upanissaye nava …pe… avigate tīṇi.
