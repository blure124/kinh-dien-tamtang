# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Tikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (1)

# 13.17. Uppannattika

**patthana13.17:1.1** Uppanno dhammo naanuppannassa dhammassa hetupaccayena paccayo. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana13.17:2.1** Hetuyā tīṇi, ārammaṇe nava, adhipatiyā nava …pe… upanissaye nava …pe… avigate tīṇi.
