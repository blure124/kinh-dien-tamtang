# Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Tikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (1)

# 13.13. Parittārammaṇattika

**patthana13.13:1.1** Parittārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naparittārammaṇo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā …. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana13.13:2.1** Hetuyā ekavīsa, ārammaṇe nava …pe… aññamaññe sattarasa …pe… avigate ekavīsa.
