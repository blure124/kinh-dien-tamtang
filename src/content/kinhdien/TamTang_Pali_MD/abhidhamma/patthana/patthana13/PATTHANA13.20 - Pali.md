# Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Tikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (1)

# 13.20. Ajjhattattika

**patthana13.20:1.1** Ajjhattaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nabahiddhā dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā.

**patthana13.20:2.1** Bahiddhā dhammaṁ paṭicca naajjhatto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā.  (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana13.20:3.1** Hetuyā dve …pe… avigate dve. (Sabbattha vitthāro.)
