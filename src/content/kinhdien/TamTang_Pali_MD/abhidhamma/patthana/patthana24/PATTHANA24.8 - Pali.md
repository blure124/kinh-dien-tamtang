# 24.8. Hetuduka, Kilesagocchaka

**patthana24.8:1.1** Nahetuṁ nakilesaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu kileso dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana24.8:2.1** Hetuyā tīṇi …pe… avigate tīṇi. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)
