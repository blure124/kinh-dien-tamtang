# 16.70. Sanidassanattika, Cittasamuṭṭhānaduka

**patthana16.70:1.1** Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naanidassanaappaṭigho nocittasamuṭṭhāno dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana16.70:2.1** Hetuyā cha, ārammaṇe tīṇi …pe… avigate cha.
