# 16.24. Kusalattika, Upādānadukādi

**patthana16.24:1.1** Akusalaṁ upādānaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naakusalo noupādāno dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana16.24:2.1** Akusalaṁ kilesaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naakusalo nokileso dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana16.24:3.1** Hetuyā pañca, ārammaṇe tīṇi …pe… avigate pañca.
