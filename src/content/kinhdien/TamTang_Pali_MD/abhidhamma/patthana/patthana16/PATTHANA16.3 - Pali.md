# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammānulomapaccanīya (3), Tikadukapaṭṭhānapāḷi (4)

# 16.3. Kusalattika, Hetusampayuttaduka

**patthana16.3:1.1** Kusalaṁ hetusampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nakusalo nahetusampayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Sahetukadukasadisaṁ.)
