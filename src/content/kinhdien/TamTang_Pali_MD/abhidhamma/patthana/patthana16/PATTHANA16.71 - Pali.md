# 16.71. Sanidassanattika, Cittasahabhūduka

**patthana16.71:1.1** Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ cittasahabhuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naanidassanaappaṭigho nocittasahabhū dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)
