# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammapaccanīyānuloma (4), Tikatikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (5)

# 23.56. Sanidassanattika, Atītattika

**patthana23.56:1.1** Nasanidassanasappaṭigho napaccuppanno dhammo anidassanaappaṭighassa paccuppannassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana23.56:2.1** Ārammaṇe cha, adhipatiyā cha, anantare samanantare tīṇi, upanissaye cha, natthiyā vigate tīṇi.
