# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammapaccanīyānuloma (4), Tikatikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (5)

# 23.16. Kusalattika, Uppannattika

**patthana23.16:1.1** Nakusalo nauppanno dhammo kusalassa uppannassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana23.16:2.1** Ārammaṇe aṭṭhārasa, adhipatiyā nava, upanissaye aṭṭhārasa.
