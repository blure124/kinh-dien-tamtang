# Dhammapaccanīyānuloma (4), Tikatikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (5)

# 23.34. Maggārammaṇattika, Kusalattika

**patthana23.34:1.1** Namaggārammaṇaṁ nakusalaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā maggārammaṇo kusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana23.34:2.1** Hetuyā pañcatiṁsa …pe… avigate pañcatiṁsa. (Naakusalaṁ naabyākataṁ natthi.)
