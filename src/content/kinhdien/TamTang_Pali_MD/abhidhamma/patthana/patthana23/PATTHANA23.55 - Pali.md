# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammapaccanīyānuloma (4), Tikatikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (5)

# 23.55. Sanidassanattika, Uppannattika

**patthana23.55:1.1** Nasanidassanasappaṭigho nauppanno dhammo anidassanaappaṭighassa uppannassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana23.55:2.1** Ārammaṇe cha, adhipatiyā upanissaye cha.
