# Paṭṭhānapakaraṇa

# Dhammapaccanīyānuloma (4), Tikatikapaṭṭhānapāḷi (5)

# 23.35. Uppannattikādi, Kusalattika

**patthana23.35:1.1** Nauppanno nakusalo dhammo uppannassa kusalassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. Ārammaṇe satta, adhipatiyā cha, upanissaye satta.

**patthana23.35:2.1** Nauppanno naakusalo dhammo uppannassa akusalassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. Ārammaṇe cha, adhipatiyā cha, upanissaye cha.

**patthana23.35:3.1** Naanupanno naabyākato dhammo uppannassa abyākatassa dhammassa hetupaccayena paccayo … tīṇi.

**patthana23.35:4.1** Nauppanno naabyākato dhammo uppannassa abyākatassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana23.35:5.1** Hetuyā tīṇi, ārammaṇe satta, adhipatiyā cattāri, sahajāte nissaye tīṇi, upanissaye satta …pe… avigate tīṇi.

**patthana23.35:6.1** Atītattikaṁ uppannattikasadisaṁ.
