# 22.20. Saṅkiliṭṭhattika, Hetuduka

**patthana22.20:1.1** Nasaṅkiliṭṭhasaṅkilesikaṁ nahetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca asaṅkiliṭṭhasaṅkilesiko hetu dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṅkhittaṁ.)

**patthana22.20:2.1** Hetuyā nava …pe… vipāke cha …pe… avigate nava.
